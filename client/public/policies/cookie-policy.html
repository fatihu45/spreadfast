Cookie and Browser Storage Policy · Last updated: 8 October 2026

1. Storage used by the service

Websites can store cookies and use local storage or session storage in your browser. SpreadFast uses local storage for sign-in tokens, with session storage as a fallback. Session storage also helps retain pending payment references and generation request identifiers so you can recover status without accidentally repeating a request.

2. Duration and controls

Local storage can remain until cleared by you or the application. Session storage normally lasts for the browser tab’s session. Signing out removes the application’s stored sign-in token. You can clear site data or restrict storage through browser settings, but doing so may sign you out or interrupt payment or generation status recovery; it does not cancel a payment or generation on the server.

3. Third-party services

Paystack checkout and linked services may use their own cookies or storage under their own notices. Media requests also disclose technical connection information to the service delivering the media. The current application code does not include a dedicated advertising-cookie or marketing-pixel integration. If non-essential tracking is introduced, its purpose and controls must be explained and consent obtained where required before activation.

4. Questions

See the Privacy Policy for data use and rights, or email tryspreadfast@gmail.com for help with browser storage.
